Case Study

Enterprise Platform Expansion

Problem

The platform served multiple state agencies with genuinely different needs, but the architecture didn't support agency-specific customization — every change was a one-size-fits-all tradeoff between enterprise priorities and individual feature requests. There was also no clear picture of who was actually using the platform or how.

Approach

Ran customer discovery across agencies to surface real requirements, then championed an architecture shift that let the platform support per-agency customization without fragmenting the codebase. Stood up product analytics — Google Tag Manager and dashboards — to measure adoption instead of guessing, and led onboarding and training as new agencies came on board.

Tech Stack
Product StrategyCustomer DiscoveryGoogle Tag ManagerAgileAzure DevOps
Outcome

Grew from roughly 1,000 to over 35,000 monthly active users, and the platform became the reference point for how to balance enterprise-wide priorities against agency-specific needs.

A Vertical SaaS Platform for Dairy Operations

Built a vertical SaaS platform for a local dairy business after customer discovery revealed that manual operational processes—not customer demand—were limiting growth. Led product strategy from problem discovery through MVP definition, prioritization, and implementation to enable scalable subscription and delivery operations.

Overview

A local dairy business owner was forced to reduce production because manual customer, subscription, and delivery management could no longer support business growth. After a previous software initiative failed, I led the product from customer discovery through MVP definition and development.

My Contributions
  • Conducted customer discovery to identify operational bottlenecks and define the highest-impact product opportunities.
  • Defined the MVP and prioritized features around customer management, subscription tracking, delivery scheduling, and operational reporting.
  • Made key product trade-offs, including excluding payment integration after determining existing UPI workflows already met customer needs.
  • Defined success metrics and dashboards to monitor business performance, including active subscriptions, milk production, customer growth, and outstanding payments.
  • Led end-to-end product development, translating business requirements into product specifications and implementation using React, TypeScript, Flask, and Python.
Outcome

The platform is being built to remove operational constraints that previously limited business growth, enabling the owner to scale customer operations through a purpose-built SaaS solution instead of manual processes.

Dota 2 AI Coach

An end-to-end system that turns raw match replays into personalized coaching feedback. Phase one — replay parsing and feature extraction — is built and shipped; everything below is the planned pipeline from structured game state through mistake detection to natural-language coaching.

Learned model Deterministic / rule-based Data structure

Not every stage here needs to be a model. Event Model and Statistics Engine stay rule-based — the logic is well-defined and doesn't benefit from being learned. Mistake Detection is a deterministic comparison against the Value Model's output, not a separate network. The plan is to spend model capacity only where the problem is genuinely ambiguous — encoding game state, modeling temporal context, predicting actions and value, and turning a detected mistake into coaching language — and keep everything else as fast, explainable code.
